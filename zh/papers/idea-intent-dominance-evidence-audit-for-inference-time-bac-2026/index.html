IDEA: Intent-Dominance Evidence Audit for Inference-Time Backdoor Detection in GUI Agents

arXiv 2026 57 method

TLDR

IDEA detects inference-time backdoor activation in GUI agents by auditing intent–evidence dominance inversion, using counterfactual probing and region ablation with finite-sample false-positive control.

评分理由

Strengths include a novel security problem, principled statistical guarantees, and extensive evaluation across five attack families with trigger localization. Weaknesses are the narrow empirical scope on a single base model and lack of broader real-world deployment evidence in the abstract.

深度分析

创新点

  • Frames backdoor activation in GUI agents as intent-evidence dominance inversion, where the instruction loses control over the decision state or an unreferenced screen region dictates it.
  • Proposes IDEA, a step-level inference-time backdoor detector for GUI agents that requires no trigger knowledge or attack data.
  • Introduces Intent Counterfactual Probing to measure how far redirected instructions move the decision state.
  • Introduces Region Causal Audit to quantify how far ablating a candidate screen region moves the decision state.
  • Uses a gated conformal score that compares both displacements against clean steps at the same distance from the clean manifold, providing a finite-sample false-positive guarantee.

方法

IDEA audits each GUI agent step at inference time without trigger knowledge or attack data. It uses Intent Counterfactual Probing to measure decision-state movement under redirected instructions and Region Causal Audit to measure movement when a candidate screen region is ablated. A gated conformal score compares these displacements against clean steps matched by distance from the clean manifold, and the approach is evaluated on Qwen2.5-VL-3B across five attack families against state-of-the-art detectors.

关键结果

On Qwen2.5-VL-3B across five attack families, IDEA consistently detects backdoor activation under a controlled false-positive rate and outperforms state-of-the-art detection methods. It also localizes the trigger region for every alarm and adds only modest inference overhead.
