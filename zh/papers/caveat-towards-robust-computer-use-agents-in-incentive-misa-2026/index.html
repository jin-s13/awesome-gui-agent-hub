CAVEAT: Towards Robust Computer-Use Agents in Incentive-Misaligned Environments

arXiv 2026 52.3 method

TLDR

Introduces CAVEAT benchmark for computer-use agents in marketplace environments with steering mechanisms, showing incentive misalignment degrades user-optimal behavior and proposing harness/post-training mitigation.

评分理由

Strengths include a novel benchmark and taxonomy of steering mechanisms, systematic evaluation across multiple model families, and actionable diagnosis of failure modes. Weaknesses are that the environments are controlled/simulated rather than real-world deployments, and the abstract is truncated, leaving some results incomplete.

深度分析

创新点

  • Introduces CAVEAT, a controlled benchmark spanning nine marketplace environments and a taxonomy of eight common steering mechanisms for incentive-misaligned environments.
  • Identifies three failure modes where steering enters the decision process: distorting user priorities, prematurely narrowing alternatives, and committing before resolving decision-relevant evidence.
  • Develops CAVEAT-Harness, an intervention targeting the diagnosed failure modes, and shows targeted post-training further improves a smaller open model.

方法

The paper evaluates five model families on CAVEAT by comparing user-optimal purchasing in matched-control episodes versus episodes with steering mechanisms enabled. It uses trajectory analysis and targeted ablations to diagnose where steering affects agent decisions, then designs and evaluates CAVEAT-Harness and targeted post-training interventions.

关键结果

Across five model families, user-optimal product purchase rates fall from 78.6% in matched-control episodes to 17.3% when steering mechanisms are enabled. Larger models and increased reasoning improve robustness, but substantial failures persist; CAVEAT-Harness raises user-optimal purchasing by 55.0%.

局限性

  • Substantial failures persist even with larger models and increased reasoning.
