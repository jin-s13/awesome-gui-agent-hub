Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Falsifiable Commitment Units (FCUs) that represent each plan step as a subgoal with a reusable skill, confirming evidence, falsifying evidence, and a confidence score.
  • A plan-test-repair loop that uses a hybrid commitment testing module, combining lightweight evidence matching with LLM-based diagnostic verification to check actions before and after execution.
  • Scope-aware repair that localizes falsification to the execution, skill, or planning level and revises only the smallest adequate part.

Methodology

FCPAgent decomposes tasks into FCUs, then executes them in a plan-test-repair loop. The hybrid testing module checks candidate actions before browser modification and observations after execution using evidence matching and LLM verification. When evidence falsifies a commitment, scope-aware repair identifies the contradiction level (execution, skill, or planning) and minimally revises that component. Evaluated on WebArena.

Key Results

On WebArena, FCPAgent achieves a 13.8% relative improvement in average success over the strongest baseline, with especially large gains on long-horizon tasks.
