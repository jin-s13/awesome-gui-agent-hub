OmegaUse-OfficeVal: Benchmarking LLM Agents on Long-Horizon Office-Suite Tasks with Economic Grounding

arXiv 2026 43.4 benchmark, application

TLDR

Introduces OmegaUse-OfficeVal, a benchmark for LLM agents on long-horizon office-suite tasks with economic grounding, comparing human vs LLM costs and quality.

Reasoning

The paper's strength lies in its novel economic grounding with human labor time and task price proxies, enabling direct cost comparisons. However, the benchmark is limited to office-suite tasks and does not specify whether agents interact via GUI, API, or other modalities, limiting generalization to other agent domains.
