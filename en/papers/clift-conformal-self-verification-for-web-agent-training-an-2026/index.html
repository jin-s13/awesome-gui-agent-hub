Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Introduces CLIFT, a training and test-time scaling method built around conformal self-verification for web agents.
  • Proposes a Compositional Conformal Certifier that keeps natural-language verification question signals whose URL-conditional evidence agrees with a training-time judge and assigns signed trust weights through polarity-aware lift.
  • Blends the self-verifier score into per-step rewards in a way that never subtracts from the judge baseline.
  • Introduces Conformal Trajectory Selection (CTS), a judge-free test-time scaling rule using a frozen certified question bank, greedy and diverse rollouts, URL-trace summaries, and conservative majority-vote swapping.
  • Shows cross-model and cross-benchmark transfer of a certified question bank, including transfer from an open model to GPT-5.5 and zero-shot improvement on a live-web agent.

Methodology

CLIFT trains the agent to answer natural-language verification questions about its own rollouts, then uses a Compositional Conformal Certifier to retain only question signals whose URL-conditional evidence agrees with a training-time judge, assign signed trust weights via polarity-aware lift, and blend the verifier score into per-step rewards without reducing the judge baseline. At test time, the frozen certified question bank is reused for Conformal Trajectory Selection, which samples a greedy rollout and diverse retries, has the self-verifier summarize each URL trace, and uses a conservative majority-vote rule to decide whether to swap trajectories without an external judge. The method is evaluated on WebArena Infinity, VisualWebArena, and Online Mind2Web.

Key Results

CLIFT achieves state-of-the-art performance among open-source web agents on WebArena Infinity. On VisualWebArena, a bank trained with an open model transfers to GPT-5.5 at test time and reaches state-of-the-art performance under the canonical harness, while on Online Mind2Web a translated certified question bank improves a live-web agent in zero-shot evaluation without training on the benchmark.
