OpenMobile-2: Building Versatile Mobile Agents with Scalable Environments and App-Native Tools

arXiv 2026 54.3 method

TLDR

OpenMobile-2 introduces open training environments (MobileGym++) with simulated commercial apps and hybrid GUI/app-native tool use, achieving competitive mobile agent performance on AndroidWorld, MobileWorld, and SPA-Bench.

Reasoning

The paper's strengths include large-scale open training data, diverse simulated environments, and a novel hybrid GUI-tool setting, with competitive results on established benchmarks. Weaknesses are that the app-native tool setting is described as experimental and real-device transfer is only briefly indicated as promising rather than fully detailed.

Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • MobileGym++: 35 realistic commercial-style simulated apps with cross-app workflows and full controllability for reset and verification, combined with newly configured Android emulator apps to span over 110 apps.
  • Open training data at scale: nearly 12K mobile interaction trajectories for supervised fine-tuning and over 2K executable tasks with automatic rewards for verifiable reinforcement learning.
  • Hybrid GUI and app-native tool use: over 300 scoped tools across 50+ apps, allowing agents to interleave GUI actions and tool calls within a task.
  • MobileGym++ Bench: a benchmark for complex, long-horizon commercial mobile scenarios supporting both GUI-only and hybrid GUI-tool evaluation on a shared task suite.

Methodology

OpenMobile-2 uses MobileGym++ and Android emulator environments to provide diverse, controllable mobile app interactions. Training combines nearly 12K interaction trajectories for supervised fine-tuning with over 2K executable tasks and automatic rewards for verifiable reinforcement learning. Evaluation covers established benchmarks AndroidWorld and MobileWorld, real-device transfer via SPA-Bench, and the new MobileGym++ Bench for GUI-only and hybrid GUI-tool settings.

Key Results

OpenMobile-2 achieves 79.9 on AndroidWorld and 50.4 on MobileWorld, and nearly doubles SPA-Bench performance from 31.9 to 59.6.
