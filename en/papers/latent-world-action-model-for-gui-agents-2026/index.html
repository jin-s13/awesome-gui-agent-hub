Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Reformulates GUI world modeling from full-screen pixel reconstruction to predicting action-induced future information in latent space.
  • Uses a large multimodal model as a latent world model with shared representations and two dedicated heads for action selection and predictive transition modeling.
  • Introduces an action-aware contrastive objective to mitigate the identity shortcut and encourage transition representations to capture action-induced changes.

Methodology

The method uses a large multimodal model as a latent world model whose shared representations feed two heads: an action head for action selection and a transition head for action-conditioned future information. An action-aware contrastive objective is added to prevent identity shortcuts by making transition representations sensitive to action-induced changes. It is evaluated against pixel-space world models in offline and online GUI settings using success rate and inference latency.

Key Results

Across offline and online GUI settings, the method achieves up to 31.9% and 40.6% relative success-rate gains over pixel-space world models, respectively, while reducing inference latency by up to 98.9%.
