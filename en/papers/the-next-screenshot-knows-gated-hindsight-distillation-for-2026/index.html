Reasoning

Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Gated Hindsight Distillation (GHD): using the next screenshot as privileged information during training, with a parameter-sharing teacher that re-scores the student's on-policy predictions and applies distillation only when the student fails and the teacher recovers the correct action.

Methodology

GHD trains a student model from interaction trajectories using prefix-action pairs, while a parameter-sharing teacher model conditions on the next screenshot to re-score the student's on-policy responses. Distillation is gated to occur only when the student's prediction is incorrect and the teacher's hindsight-conditioned scoring recovers the demonstrated action. The method is evaluated on AndroidWorld and AndroidLab against GRPO using two vision-language models.

Key Results

GHD improves task success over GRPO on both AndroidWorld and AndroidLab across two vision-language models.
