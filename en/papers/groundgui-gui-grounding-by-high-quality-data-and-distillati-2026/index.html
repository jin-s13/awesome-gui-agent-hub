Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Screen Referring Grounding Dataset (ScreenRef), a large-scale GUI grounding dataset with 586K screenshots and over 633K referring expression instances across desktop, web, and mobile domains.
  • Distillation-to-Reinforcement Learning (D2RL), a training method designed to address limited generalization from supervised fine-tuning and sparse reward issues in reinforcement learning.
  • GroundGUI-8B, a GUI grounding model based on Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct trained with sequential supervised fine-tuning and D2RL.

Methodology

The authors construct ScreenRef, a GUI grounding dataset of screenshots paired with referring expressions and box or point annotations across desktop, web, and mobile. They train Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct using sequential supervised fine-tuning followed by the proposed D2RL method. The resulting model is evaluated on ScreenSpot-V2, ScreenSpot-Pro, OSWorld-G, MMBench-GUI L2, and UI-Vision.

Key Results

GroundGUI-8B achieves 95.2% on ScreenSpot-V2, 63.9% on ScreenSpot-Pro, 65.4% on OSWorld-G, 86.8% on MMBench-GUI L2, and 38.3% on UI-Vision. Among 8B-scale models, it substantially outperforms models trained on open-source datasets and is comparable or superior to models trained on closed-source data.
