Guides That Cause Actions: An Offline Study of Guide-Action Mutual Reinforcement in Multimodal Web Agents

arXiv 2026 61.7 method

TLDR

Presents WebMRE, an offline deterministic benchmark from WebArena trajectories, showing guide sentences causally improve action selection in multimodal web agents.

Reasoning

Strengths include a fully audited offline benchmark enabling reproducible scoring and a rigorous mediation analysis showing that guide sentences carry causal instruction meaning. Weaknesses are that the benchmark is derived only from successful trajectories, lacks live-environment validation, and optimizing the guide-conditioned reward did not improve performance.

Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • WebMRE, an offline benchmark of 541 tasks and 5,293 steps derived from successful WebArena trajectories with fully audited test labels and deterministic, environment-free scoring.
  • First study of the mutual reinforcement effect between human-oriented guide sentences and grounded actions in multimodal web agents.
  • Mediation analysis showing the guide is a causal channel carrying instruction meaning rather than only the label string.
  • A replayable offline reward signal enabled by the deterministic protocol.

Methodology

The authors construct WebMRE from successful WebArena trajectories, pairing 541 tasks and 5,293 steps with guide sentences and grounded actions under audited labels. They evaluate Qwen3.5 models in both decoding orders across three seeds, perform mediation analyses by forcing gold, swapped, or paraphrased guides as prefixes, and compare fine-tuned models against zero-shot commercial baselines.

Key Results

Jointly decoding a guide improves element selection over an action-only reference by 0.9 to 2.2 points depending on model scale and decoding order. Forcing a gold guide lifts action accuracy from .422 to .684, another step's guide collapses it to .055, and a paraphrase recovers half the gain; fine-tuned models outperform GPT-5.5, Claude Opus 4.8, and Gemini 3.5 Flash on every offline metric.

Limitations

  • Optimizing the offline reward from a strong checkpoint brings no gain yet.
