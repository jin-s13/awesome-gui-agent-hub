Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Introduces GUI-CC, a benchmark that evaluates contextual consistency of GUI world models as multi-step agent environments rather than isolated next-screen predictors.
  • Provides two complementary evaluation tracks: an offline reference-action track that rolls models along real mobile GUI trajectories, and an online agent-loop track where fixed probing agents interact with model-generated UIs.
  • Constructs 500 offline trajectory tasks from GUIOdyssey and 200 emulator-verified online tasks across 30 mobile apps.
  • Evaluates models along multiple dimensions: transition fidelity, transition plausibility, contextual consistency, and task progress.

Methodology

GUI-CC evaluates GUI world models by rolling them along real mobile GUI trajectories in an offline reference-action track and by letting fixed probing agents interact with model-generated UIs in an online agent-loop track. The benchmark includes 500 offline trajectory tasks from GUIOdyssey and 200 emulator-verified online tasks across 30 mobile apps, measuring transition fidelity, transition plausibility, contextual consistency, and task progress.

Key Results

Experiments show that plausible single-step generation does not guarantee reliable environment simulation: current models often produce usable-looking screens while failing to preserve task-relevant context or support executable multi-step rollouts.

Limitations

  • Current GUI world models often fail to preserve task-relevant context or support executable multi-step rollouts despite producing plausible single-step screens.
  • The benchmark is scoped to mobile GUI tasks, using 30 mobile apps and offline trajectories derived from GUIOdyssey, so the findings may not generalize to other GUI domains.
