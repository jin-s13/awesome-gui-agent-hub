Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Nested double-loop recursive improvement framework with an outer world-data-model flywheel and an inner data flywheel
  • Open-world seeding that mines authentic workflows as seed tasks from instructional videos
  • Visual-semantic dual-path trajectory reconstruction
  • Autocatalytic propagation that transforms seeds into reusable primitives for controllable synthesis of diverse, complex, and verifiable tasks
  • Feedback-guided snapshot-rollback retry mechanism for efficient and reliable trajectory collection in real environments

Methodology

CUA-Master uses a nested double-loop recursive improvement framework: an outer world-data-model flywheel incorporates open-world corpus and uses model weaknesses to guide data construction and optimization, while an inner flywheel couples open-world seeding from instructional videos with autocatalytic propagation of reusable primitives for task synthesis. It includes visual-semantic dual-path trajectory reconstruction and a feedback-guided snapshot-rollback retry mechanism for trajectory collection. The resulting CUA-Master-27B is evaluated on GUI-only and GUI-CLI hybrid benchmarks including OSWorld Verified, OSWorld 2.0, WeaveBench, and Agents' Last Exam.

Key Results

CUA-Master-27B achieves competitive performance, scoring 80.2 on OSWorld Verified, 36.9 on OSWorld 2.0, 43.9 on WeaveBench, and 25.0 on Agents' Last Exam. The framework also substantially improves data synthesis quality and efficiency and exhibits continuous gains across recursive iterations.
