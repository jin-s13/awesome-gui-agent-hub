Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Scalable environment-grounded training stack with closed-loop data engine for automated task generation, rollout, filtering, capability balancing, SFT, and online RL across parallel environments
  • In-context demonstration learning that transforms multimodal demonstrations into flexible subtask-level workflows and re-plans from the live interface
  • OSWorkerBench benchmark with 100 long-horizon office tasks across 41 applications, featuring self-demo and variant-demo settings for demonstration-guided evaluation

Methodology

UI-Mate integrates a closed-loop training pipeline that generates tasks, constructs environments, collects rollouts, filters data, balances capabilities, and performs SFT and online RL at scale, combined with in-context demonstration learning that converts multimodal demonstrations into subtask workflows and replans from the live GUI. The model is evaluated on general computer-use benchmarks (OSWorld-Verified, WindowsAgentArena) and the new OSWorkerBench, which supports instruction-only and demonstration-guided evaluation with self-demo and variant-demo subsets.

Key Results

UI-Mate-27B achieves new open-weight SOTA on general benchmarks (77.0% OSWorld-Verified, 66.2% WindowsAgentArena), and on OSWorkerBench reaches 41.0% strict success and 76.9% progress, outperforming its base model by 17.7 and 24.5 points. On the self-demo subset, one demonstration raises strict success from 17.2% to 35.4% and progress from 67.9% to 81.1%.
